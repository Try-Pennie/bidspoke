# Bidspoke — Design Context

## Product
A Pipedream-style workflow automation builder, tailored for Pennie's partner bid management use case. Handles lead routing, credit pulls, partner bidding decisions, and API integrations. Real money moves through these workflows.

## Target Audience
Small ops team: ~2 engineers and ~2 non-technical operations people. Everyone uses it with focus time (not glancing mid-meeting). Non-technical users need to understand what's happening without needing a tutorial.

## Use Cases
- Build and edit workflow automations on the canvas
- Monitor execution history and debug failures
- Manage environment variables and API sources
- Watch for workflow health (are runs succeeding?)

## Brand Personality — Direction B: Modern & Confident
Polished and trustworthy but not stuffy. Clear visual hierarchy, generous whitespace, a strong accent color used sparingly. Feels like it was designed, not assembled. Approachable for non-technical ops people, but precise and purposeful enough to feel trustworthy when real financial decisions are being automated.

## Tone
Professional but human. Not enterprise-bloated. Not startup-cute. The equivalent of a well-dressed colleague who explains things clearly and doesn't waste your time.

## Colour
- Navy `#1D212F` marks a selection: a chosen list row, an active tab's underline, a selected segment or chip.
- Blue `#1B72BA` is for buttons only, never for a selection or a status.
- Red is reserved for something genuinely wrong (a missing credential, a refused save, a failed write). Not for a warning that needs no action, and not for decoration.

- Grey hint and secondary text is the `hint` token `#697081` (`text-hint`), never `text-gray-500`, which fails AA on the beige page (4.48 to 1). It is the lightest grey of that hue clearing 4.5 to 1 on beige, gray-100 and white (Dan, 2026-10-16, #2144). The shared `Field` draws its hint in it.
- Red text goes through `FieldError` in `components/ui/form.tsx`, the red mark through `ProblemMark`.
- A focus ring is not a selection or a button: it marks where the keyboard is and stays blue as the design system draws it.
- An open dropdown's highlighted row (the one under the mouse or the arrow keys) is pale grey, the `highlight` token `#F1EFEA`, behind the usual dark text, in every dropdown. The chosen row keeps its navy check. Settled by Dan on 2026-10-21 in #2338 round 27, and held by `apps/web/src/lib/__guards__/dropdown-highlight-guard.test.ts`.
- On a highlighted row, an option's grey second line is the `hint-on-highlight` token `#666D7E` (4.51 to 1 on the highlight, where `hint` measures 4.31); every other grey hint stays `hint`. Settled by Dan on 2026-10-21 in #2338 round 28.
- A box holds only what is typed. Fixed words of a value (a unit such as "% of debt") sit beside the box, never inside it as if typed (Dan, 2026-10-17, #2268).

Settled in the partners page design rounds (#2114, 2026-10-14) as a rule for the whole app. It holds on the partners page now (#2144, enforced by `apps/web/src/lib/__tests__/partner-page-colour-guard.test.ts`); the rest of the app is #2126.

## Copy
- No line of text ends with a single word on a line of its own. When a sentence wraps and leaves one word behind, reword it or rebalance the line until the last line holds at least two words. This holds for every line of Bidspoke copy (headings, notices, hints, buttons, table cells) at the width it is drawn at.

Made by Dan on 2026-10-20 in the #2338 Connections page design rounds, as a rule for all of Bidspoke's copy. The settled Connections design checks it on every frame (`docs/design/connections-page.checks.js`, decision D27).

## Controls
- A box that needs an ID from another tool takes whatever a person would copy from that tool, such as a whole Google Drive address, and reads the ID out of it itself. It never asks for the part of an address that holds the ID. Made by Dan on 2026-10-21 in #2338 design round 29 as a rule for all of Bidspoke; first held by the Google list's shared drive box (`readDriveAddress`).
- A working line's spinner is level with the capitals. The shared `.outcome svg` rule is the only thing that sizes it (Dan, 2026-10-21, #2338 round 32), held by `apps/web/src/lib/__guards__/outcome-spinner-guard.test.ts`.
- A dropdown's open menu sits over what is below it, never pushing the page down (Dan, 2026-10-21, #2338 round 34).
- Every dialog's title is 20px, drawn by the shared `.dialog-title` rule alone (Dan, 2026-10-21, #2338 round 35).

## Key Reference
Pipedream's workflow builder — but more focused, more opinionated about the bid management use case.
