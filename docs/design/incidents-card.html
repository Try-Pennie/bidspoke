Incidents card: the settled design (#2101)

Settled with Dan in four rendered design rounds on 2026-10-14, one question a round. Built in apps/web/src/components/dashboard/IncidentsSection.tsx (the card), apps/web/src/components/dashboard/OngoingIncidents.tsx (the line), apps/web/src/lib/incidents.ts (their words) and the worker routes GET /api/incidents and GET /api/incidents/ongoing.

Decision 1: grouped by day (round 1)

Incidents sit under a day heading (Today, Yesterday, then the weekday and date), so a bad day with several stands out and each time needs only the hour; the first heading says the times are ET. Each row is the time range ("2:34 PM to 4:06 PM", or "3:00 PM, end not recorded"), the signal, the subject, the duration from the span rule the Slack all-clear uses, and the Slack pages. A six column table and a two line list were rendered and set aside.

Decision 2: at the bottom of the dashboard (round 2)

After By workflow and Recent failures, so the page reads summary first and history last. At the top, and under the success rate, were rendered and set aside; what is urgent now moved to its own line instead (decision 4).

Decision 3: the card follows the page's 7, 14, 30 day switch

No separate 90 day view. An incident is in the card when it started in the window or ended in it, so one that began before the window and ended inside it still shows. With nothing in the window the card says "No incidents in the last N days", or "No incidents since recording began on Oct 14" when the window reaches back before the record began.

Decision 4: an ongoing incident is one line under the page header (round 3)

An open incident is not a row in the card. Each one is a line under the page header, above the success rate: "Ongoing incident", then the signal on the subject, since when (with its day when it began before today), how long so far, and its Slack pages. It is seen first and stated once; the card holds only incidents that have closed. A marked row in its day, and an Ongoing heading of its own in the card, were rendered and set aside.

Decision 5: the ongoing line is red (round 4)

The colour the dashboard gives failures, because an ongoing incident is something wrong right now: red 50 ground, red 200 border, the label in red 700, the sentence in graphite. The card stays neutral like By workflow, its day headings in the table header treatment on beige. Amber (the config warning colour) and blue (the dashboard accent) were rendered and set aside.

States the drawing does not show

The card has a loading skeleton, a failed read shown as an error with a Retry (never as empty), and the empty sentence above. A failed read of the ongoing incidents is its own neutral error line with a Retry, so it never reads as "no ongoing incident".

Where the build departs from the drawing

The day headings are gray 600, not the table header's gray 500: gray 500 on beige measures 4.49:1, under the 4.5:1 minimum for text. With an ongoing incident shown above and nothing closed in the window, the empty card says "No other incidents", so it does not contradict the line.

What is the rendering, not the design

The dashboard is drawn at a 1280px laptop width and scaled to fit this page; the incidents in it are invented samples; and it uses the system font stack rather than the app's Geist. None of these were decided.
