Bid matrix alert: the settled design (#1864)

Settled with Dan in three rendered design rounds on 2026-10-07, after a teammate asked for the matrix name and the person who made the change. Drawn from two real edits recovered from the Salesforce recycle bin, with the editor's name replaced. Built in apps/worker/src/lib/matrix-audit-message.ts.

Decision 1: the changes are a grid, not a list

Debt bands down the side, Vantage bands across, as the matrix looks in Salesforce. Only the rows and columns that changed are drawn, and a cell that did not change is a dot. The old list, one line per bid cut off at 20, hid 16 of the 36 bids changed on 2026-10-07; a line per Debt band and a single sentence were also rendered and set aside.

Decision 2: a cell is the new bid with how far it moved

$14 (-8): the bid that is live now, and the size of the move, which is what the reader judges. Old and new ($22→$14) made the reader do the subtraction; the move alone (-$8) hid what is live. A bid that appeared reads $14 (new) and one that was deleted reads gone.

Decision 3: one fixed title, the facts in labelled lines

The title is always "Bid matrix changed", so the alert is recognisable at a glance in the channel. Under it, labelled lines: Matrix (the partner's name, and regular or sell-off), Changed by (who, and the span of their saves in ET, dated when not today), Bids changed (the count). Putting the matrix or the person in the title, or folding the facts into one sentence, were rendered and set aside.

Decision 4: one message per matrix

Each message has one Matrix line, so two matrices edited in the same hour post as two messages.

What is the rendering medium, not the design

The Slack window, avatar, day dividers and compose box below are a stand in for Slack so the alert is seen in its channel; Slack draws them itself. The grid is a Slack code block, so its alignment comes from Slack's monospace font, and the link is a Slack mrkdwn link.
