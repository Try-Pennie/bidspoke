Canvas wires: the settled design (#1570)

Settled with Dan in two rendered design rounds on 2026-09-25, from the real layout of the Liberty1 click handler and the Engine Bidding buyer select card. Built in apps/web/src/components/canvas/edges/edge-routes.ts.

Decision 1: one shared turn line, wires into a card join

Every wire runs down its handle's column, turns on one line 32 px above its target (half the gap when the gap is tighter), and drops in, so wires into the same card join on that line. Chosen over the staggered heights #1489 shipped, which gave every wire into a card its own height and read as a staircase under a card with seven outputs, and over landing each wire at its own point on the card. The routes stay distinguishable by their separate drops from each handle.

Decision 2: a hop where a wire crosses another

With one shared line, a wire bound for another card can cross a drop (unknown crossing the Already sent drop). It arches over it, 9 px radius, so a crossing is never read as a join. Chosen over a plain crossing and a gap in the line. Converge wires into a parallel end follow the same two rules.

Idioms of this page, not of the design

  • Drawn in SVG at 50% of canvas size; the canvas draws the same geometry at full size through React Flow.
  • The dot grid and card chrome approximate the canvas so the wires are judged in context; they are not specified here.
